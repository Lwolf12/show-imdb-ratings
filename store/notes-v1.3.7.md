**What's new in 1.3.7**

- Netflix: a card whose name belongs to both a well-known film and a well-known series (Hannibal) now shows the right one. The extension reads that title's public Netflix page once to learn whether it is a film or a series.
- Rotten Tomatoes and Metacritic scores come from OMDb, which has them mostly for films; most TV shows will not show them.

Download the ZIP, extract it, then in Chrome open chrome://extensions, turn on Developer mode, click **Load unpacked** and pick the `show-imdb-ratings` folder. Details in the [README](https://github.com/Lwolf12/show-imdb-ratings#readme).
