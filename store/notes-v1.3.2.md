**What's new in 1.3.2**

- Netflix: the "Coming This Week", "Coming Next Week" and "Worth the Wait" rows on New & Popular get their badges. They were left out before because a title not out yet can share a name with an older film and show that film's rating; that can still happen on a card, and the hover preview, which knows the year, corrects it.

Download the ZIP, extract it, then in Chrome open chrome://extensions, turn on Developer mode, click **Load unpacked** and pick the `show-imdb-ratings` folder. Details in the [README](https://github.com/Lwolf12/show-imdb-ratings#readme).
