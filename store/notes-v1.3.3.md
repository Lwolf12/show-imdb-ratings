**What's new in 1.3.3**

- Card badges are easier to read on light or busy artwork: their background is darker (75% black, was 50%), so a white edge, fine detail or yellow art no longer swallows the digits. A little of the artwork still shows through.

Download the ZIP, extract it, then in Chrome open chrome://extensions, turn on Developer mode, click **Load unpacked** and pick the `show-imdb-ratings` folder. Details in the [README](https://github.com/Lwolf12/show-imdb-ratings#readme).
