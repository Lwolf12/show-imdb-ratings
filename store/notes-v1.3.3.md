**What's new in 1.3.3**

- Card badges are easier to read on light or busy artwork. The artwork behind a badge is now blurred and dimmed under its see-through ground, so a white edge, fine detail or yellow art no longer swallows the digits. On dark posters the badges look the same as before.

Download the ZIP, extract it, then in Chrome open chrome://extensions, turn on Developer mode, click **Load unpacked** and pick the `show-imdb-ratings` folder. Details in the [README](https://github.com/Lwolf12/show-imdb-ratings#readme).
