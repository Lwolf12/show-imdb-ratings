**What's new in 1.3.0**

- Letterboxd: tick it under Sites and the browser asks to allow letterboxd.com; from then on every poster on its browse pages, lists, watchlists, diaries and profiles carries the IMDb rating, and a film's own page gets a chip under the credits. Off until you tick it, like every site added from now on, so an update never switches the extension off for anyone.
- Sites is now a list, with a row under it to add an address of your own: a country's Amazon store (www.amazon.co.uk/gp/video), or a site that moved. Pick the site whose pages it serves, click Add, allow the address when the browser asks, and it gets a row and a tick of its own. The cross on the row removes it, permission included.
- Prime Video, on the rest of amazon.com: once www.amazon.com is added as an address under Prime Video, the shop's search results get badges too, on the films and shows among them (Prime Video titles and discs; books and the rest are left alone), with the disc's format tag taken off the name. A disc's own page in Movies & TV gets a chip under its name, and its "Customers also bought" and sponsored rows get badges on their images.
- Prime Video: the "Featured Originals and Exclusives" cards keep their rating when one expands. The expanded panel put a Play link first, which the extension read as the title, so an expanded card showed another film's rating or none. The cards also tell series from films now, so a new series no longer borrows an old film's rating.
- Prime Video: the "Top 10" row gets its ratings. Those cards are laid out differently, and the badge landed far off the page.
- A title named as a version, like "Blade Runner: The Final Cut", finds the film.
- Settings: unticking Refresh automatically now says what Update now does (rebuild the database) instead of "use it when you want".

Download the ZIP, extract it, then in Chrome open chrome://extensions, turn on Developer mode, click **Load unpacked** and pick the `show-imdb-ratings` folder. Details in the [README](https://github.com/Lwolf12/show-imdb-ratings#readme).
