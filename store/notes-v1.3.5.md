**What's new in 1.3.5**

- Letterboxd's poster badges now follow the badge size under Badges on title cards, one step smaller than on the streaming sites since its posters are small: 8, 9 or 11 px for Small, Medium and Large. Medium is the size they had before; only the tiniest posters (under 60 px, such as a member's favourites) keep their fixed score-only badge.

Download the ZIP, extract it, then in Chrome open chrome://extensions, turn on Developer mode, click **Load unpacked** and pick the `show-imdb-ratings` folder. Details in the [README](https://github.com/Lwolf12/show-imdb-ratings#readme).
