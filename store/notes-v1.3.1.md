**What's new in 1.3.1**

- Card badges come in three sizes under Badges on title cards: Small, Medium and Large. Medium, the new default, is smaller and more see-through than before, so the rating is there when you look for it rather than the first thing on the page; Large is the solid badge of 1.3.0, which reads best on bright artwork. The samples beside the label choice show each size as you pick it.
- Settings: with the extension on two synced browsers, an address added in one no longer gets switched off by a click in the other, which could not allow it.
- Amazon shop pages: a film whose name has a word like Season in it ("Season of the Witch") is looked up as a film, not a series; a sponsored row's names lose their "Sponsored Ad" prefix.
- Card badges with the short scores on: a badge half out of the window no longer keeps re-measuring itself while it sits there, and badges that leave the page are let go.
- The add row under Sites guesses Apple TV for a tv.apple.com address.
- Under the hood: the toolbar percentage during a build is set without re-reading the settings each step, and the names from Amazon's rows have tests.

Download the ZIP, extract it, then in Chrome open chrome://extensions, turn on Developer mode, click **Load unpacked** and pick the `show-imdb-ratings` folder. Details in the [README](https://github.com/Lwolf12/show-imdb-ratings#readme).
